What you get

Everything you actually watch for, nothing that watches back.

A full viewing client — search, player, subscriptions, playlists, history — built on a deliberately small and boring stack so one person can keep maintaining it.

  • Search & watch

    Full search, channel pages, regional trending and shorts, with a Vidstack player that has picture-in-picture and keyboard shortcuts.

  • Two upstreams, one shape

    Piped and Invidious are normalized into a single unified API. Either can be disabled, and each user can point at their own instances.

  • Accounts that stay local

    Auth.js with credentials and bcrypt, multi-user out of the box. No third-party identity provider is ever contacted.

  • Playlists & history

    Local playlists, watch history, likes and saves, plus a personal stats dashboard — all rows in your own database file.

  • Import & export

    Bring years of YouTube Takeout history in, and take everything back out as JSON whenever you want. No lock-in by design.

  • Installable, anywhere

    A PWA with manifest and service worker, a Docker Compose setup with healthchecks, and a work-in-progress TV client.

How it works

Three hops, and none of them belong to Google.

Every video request funnels through one proxy layer with keep-alive connection pooling, response caching and rate limiting in front of it.

  • You ask

    The browser calls your own server over end-to-end typed tRPC. No request ever leaves for an analytics or ad endpoint, because there aren't any.

  • The proxy fetches

    Your server talks to Piped and Invidious — public instances or the ones you host — and normalizes their two different shapes into one unified model.

  • SQLite remembers

    Responses are cached and your signals are written to a single SQLite file you can back up, move or delete. That file is the whole of your data.

Recommendations

A feed built from your history, not from other people's.

The recommender is content-based only — there is no collaborative filtering, so nothing about you is ever inferred from, or compared against, anyone else. It reads what you watched, builds a small model of it, and diversifies the result so the feed doesn't collapse into one topic.

On a cold start it simply falls back to trending, then gets more yours with every video.

  • signals Collect watch history
  • tf-idf Model titles and tags
  • scoring Rank the candidates
  • mmr Diversify with maximal marginal relevance
  • coldstart Fall back to trending when empty
Self-host

Up and running in five commands.

Node 22+ and a reachable Piped or Invidious instance. That's the whole dependency list.

~/owntube 
$ cp apps/web/.env.example apps/web/.env
$ corepack enable && corepack prepare pnpm@9.15.9 --activate
$ pnpm install
$ pnpm run db:migrate
$ pnpm dev

# open http://localhost:3000, register on /register
# or: docker compose up --build

Deploying on Unraid or running a fully local Invidious? Both paths are documented in docs/.
